"""Balance and tracking feedback that uses the measured ground reaction.

The flat-floor controller assumes the ground normal is world-vertical. On a
slope that assumption is wrong in several places. Each remedy needs only the
contact force and moment at the wheel (``contact_sensing``). They are listed
by how much the tuning sweep found they matter (results/offroad/tuning):

1. **Where to lean.** A wheel standing on a grade touches the ground uphill of
   its centre, so the whole machine must lean until its centre of mass is over
   that contact point. For this wheel the lean is almost as large as the grade
   itself. Without it the position loop has to supply the lean as a standing
   error, roughly 0.7 m of sag on an 8 degree grade. This is the one that
   decides the outcome: a lean 10% too small or too large is measurably worse.
2. **What torque holds it there.** The weight acting through the offset
   contact point loads the shaft and the loaded shell. That static torque is
   computed from the sensed normal and fed forward, so feedback only handles
   deviations. Removing it costs less than removing the lean.
3. **Shaft angle that agrees with position.** Rolling without slip ties
   position, pitch and shaft angle together. Distance travelled along
   undulating ground breaks an integrated shaft-angle reference, so the shaft
   error is taken from the position error instead. A small effect.
4. **Whether it is on the ground at all.** A handover between surfaces can
   leave one controller interval with no contact. The scheduled gain, the
   load shares and the rolling reference of the last loaded interval are kept
   through it. Torques also fade if the filtered normal load stays below a
   threshold; no run in the suite ever reached it.

Measuring the gain schedule's phase against the sensed normal instead of
vertical is available (``schedule_on_ground_normal``) and off: the sweep found
no benefit from it, and on the Mars yard it made falls more frequent.

Heading is free by default. On steep rough ground it matters: the wheel holds
a grade along its shaft-driven direction with the shaft (43 deg bound) but
across it only with a shell (16.7 deg) or a belt cap (7.8 deg), and the flat
library uses the unloaded shell as a roll reaction wheel whose reaction turns
the platform. ``heading_reference='fall_line'`` with shell steering keeps the
shaft-driven direction on the fall line of the sensed ground; it is off by
default and evaluated in results/gazebo/mars_yaw_study.

Two things were tried and are deliberately absent. Scaling torque to keep the
implied tangential force inside an estimated friction cone made the wheel
fall: a balancing machine's holding torque is not optional, and cutting it to
protect traction lets the body topple. The friction estimate is therefore
reported but does not limit any command. Freezing the sensed normal while the
support crosses the equatorial gap left it stale whenever the wheel lingered
there; it is slowed instead.

The feedback gains themselves are the unchanged flat-floor library. Nothing
here adds an external force, resets a state or moves the requested target.
In ``terrain_aware`` the state feedback and the contact wrench are simulator
ground truth. ``momentum_observer`` is the same controller without the force
sensor: the force and moment behind the normal and the load fraction come from
a momentum observer (``MomentumObserver``) that reads only the state, the drive
torques and the model. In both, the per-surface loads used for gain blending
and rim detection, the rolling feedforward and the incoming-drive preview
still read the simulator's contacts.

``hardware`` reads nothing a real robot could not measure. The plant it is
given is a ``StateMirror``: the measured state (motion capture for the fork,
encoders for the joints) and the inputs sent, with collisions switched off and
no ground. The observer's force and moment locate the support
(``SupportLocator``): which shell, belt or rim carries the load, where and how
much. Those estimated contacts replace the solver's in the gain blending, rim
detection, holding-torque split and rolling feedforward, and the plane through
the estimated support point with the sensed normal replaces the ground plane in
the incoming-drive preview.
"""

# State weights of the flat-floor LQR (control.design_lqr), in its scaled
# coordinates. Order: x y z roll pitch yaw shaft shell_A shell_B, then the
# nine velocities. Kept here only so a variant can override some entries;
# design_weighted_lqr with no overrides reproduces design_lqr exactly.
_FLAT_Q = (2., 2., 1., 30., 30., .01, .001, .001, .001, 2., 2., .1, 4., 4., .05, .01, .01, .01)
_FLAT_SCALES = (.15, .15, .005, .15, .15, .5, 1., 1., 1., 1., 1., .1, 1., 1., 1., 10., 10., 10.)
_FLAT_R = (.3, .3, .3, 1., 1.)

# Gain-library variants: state-weight overrides by index.
#
# 'flat' is the library as designed for the level floor. Its heading weight
# (0.01) and shell-speed weights (0.01 at 10 rad/s) are close to zero, so the
# LQR is free to use the unloaded shell as a roll reaction wheel: at the
# ordinary phase that shell's roll gain is over half the loaded shell's. A
# reaction wheel only gives transient torque. Under a lasting roll or lateral
# error (a side slope, a facet) the unloaded shell keeps accelerating, and its
# axis is tilted by the carrier phase, so its reaction turns the platform. On
# the Mars yard that is the main source of heading drift.
#
# 'yaw_aware' also weights heading, yaw rate and both shell speeds. The
# unloaded shell then keeps a small roll share, parks near zero speed, damps
# yaw, and at a pole (both shells free, axes near vertical) acts as a yaw
# reaction wheel. In the linear model this cuts heading drift under a lasting
# side load ten- to forty-fold. In Gazebo on the Mars yard it fell on all 12
# slope starts (results/gazebo/mars_yaw_study): the unloaded shell's roll
# help is part of what holds the wheel on a side slope, and without it the
# loaded shell saturates. It is kept for comparison, not recommended; steer
# heading with heading_reference='fall_line' and the shells instead.
LQR_WEIGHTS = {
    'flat': None,
    'yaw_aware': {5: 5., 14: 5., 15: 1., 16: 1., 17: 1.},
}


def design_weighted_lqr(plant, equilibrium, overrides=None, epsilon=1e-5):
    """``control.design_lqr`` with some diagonal state weights replaced.

    Same linearisation, scaling, reachable-subspace reduction and input
    weights; ``overrides`` maps state index to a new weight.
    """
    A, B, f0 = linearize(plant, equilibrium, epsilon)
    scales = np.array(_FLAT_SCALES)
    q = np.array(_FLAT_Q)
    for index, weight in (overrides or {}).items():
        q[int(index)] = float(weight)
    As = A*scales[None, :]/scales[:, None]
    Bs = B*plant.bounds[None, :]/scales[:, None]
    reach = Bs.copy()
    for _ in range(A.shape[0]):
        reach = np.column_stack((reach, As@reach[:, -Bs.shape[1]:]))
    U, s, _ = np.linalg.svd(reach, full_matrices=False)
    rank = int(np.sum(s > max(s[0]*1e-8, 1e-10)))
    T, Tn = U[:, :rank], U[:, rank:]
    omitted = np.linalg.eigvals(Tn.T@As@Tn) if Tn.size else np.array([])
    if np.any(np.abs(omitted) > 1.00001):
        raise RuntimeError(f'Unstabilizable growing modes in local model: {omitted}')
    Ac, Bc = T.T@As@T, T.T@Bs
    R = np.diag(_FLAT_R)
    P = solve_discrete_are(Ac, Bc, T.T@np.diag(q)@T, R)
    Kc = np.linalg.solve(R+Bc.T@P@Bc, Bc.T@P@Ac)
    K = plant.bounds[:, None]*(Kc@T.T)/scales[None, :]
    closed = np.linalg.eigvals(A-B@K)
    return {'A': A, 'B': B, 'K': K, 'f0': f0, 'keep': np.arange(A.shape[0]), 'reachable_rank': rank,
            'reach_singular_values': s, 'discarded_eigenvalues': omitted,
            'open_eigenvalues': np.linalg.eigvals(A), 'closed_eigenvalues': closed,
            'epsilon': float(epsilon), 'state_scales': scales, 'state_weights': q,
            'method': 'full_state_lqr', 'full_closed_spectral_radius': float(max(abs(closed)))}


def _variant_cache_path(cache_path, weights):
    """Separate cache file per weight set; the name carries a hash of the weights.

    The library fingerprint covers model and sources but not these weights,
    so a changed weight set must not find the old file.
    """
    tag = hashlib.sha256(json.dumps({'q': _FLAT_Q, 'overrides': LQR_WEIGHTS[weights]},
                                    sort_keys=True).encode()).hexdigest()[:10]
    path = Path(cache_path)
    return path.with_name(f'{path.stem}_{weights}_{tag}{path.suffix}')


# 'terrain_aware' reads the solved contact force and moment (simulation only);
# 'momentum_observer' estimates them from the motion; 'hardware' also estimates
# the loaded surface, contact point and ground plane, reading no contact at all;
# 'flat_baseline' ignores the ground.
CONTROLLER_MODES = ('terrain_aware', 'momentum_observer', 'hardware', 'flat_baseline')
OBSERVER_MODES = ('momentum_observer', 'hardware')


@dataclass
class OffroadParams:
    """Controller settings. ``flat_baseline`` ignores every terrain option.

    ``momentum_observer`` is ``terrain_aware`` with the ground force and moment
    estimated from the motion by ``MomentumObserver`` instead of read from the
    contact solver, so it needs no force sensor. ``hardware`` also estimates
    which surface is loaded, where, and the ground plane, from the same force
    and moment, and runs on a ``StateMirror`` that holds only measurements.

    The defaults are the outcome of the tuning sweep saved in
    results/offroad/tuning/tuning.json.
    """
    mode: str = 'terrain_aware'            # 'terrain_aware', 'momentum_observer', 'hardware' or 'flat_baseline'
    synchronize: bool = True               # prepare incoming drives, as in the lab
    normal_source: str = 'intrinsic'       # 'intrinsic' (from force and moment) or 'solver' (terrain_aware only)
    observer_cutoff_rad_s: float = 100.    # bandwidth of the momentum observer (momentum_observer, hardware)
    support_min_load_fraction: float = .05  # hardware: an observed load below this fraction of weight is no contact
    normal_time_constant_s: float = .02    # low-pass on the sensed ground normal
    rim_aware: bool = True                 # treat the equatorial rims as rims; False reads them as sphere
    rim_time_constant_s: float = .2        # low-pass along the carrier axis while the support is on the rims
    rim_hold_speed_m_s: float = .02        # resting on both rims below this speed, the axial part is held
    gap_margin_m: float = .002             # treat this far beyond the gap as rim support too
    lean_gain: float = 1.                  # 1 puts the centre of mass over the contact point
    support_feedforward: float = 1.        # 1 applies the full static holding torque
    schedule_on_ground_normal: bool = False  # True measures the gain phase against the sensed normal
    shaft_error: str = 'rolling'           # 'material', 'free' or 'rolling'
    rolling_allocation: str = 'least_squares'  # 'damped', 'bounded', 'feasible' are opt-in experiments
    rolling_damping_m_s: float = .01       # penalty per normalized drive rate for 'damped'
    rolling_speed_limits: tuple = (20., 20., 20., .3, .3)  # assumed reference limits: rad/s, rad/s, rad/s, m/s, m/s
    rolling_acceleration_limits: tuple = (100., 100., 100., 3., 3.)  # assumed reference limits per second
    rolling_effort_m_s: float = .001       # normalized speed cost for 'bounded'; not electrical effort
    rolling_change_m_s: float = .001       # normalized reference-change cost for 'bounded'
    rolling_adaptive_effort: bool = False  # increase speed cost as estimated horizontal authority vanishes
    rolling_weak_effort_m_s: float = .005
    rolling_authority_m_s: float = .02     # normalized Jacobian singular value for half of the cost increase
    rolling_reference_catchup_gain_s_inv: float = 1.
    rolling_reference_catchup_speed_m_s: float = .06
    rolling_reference_position_limit_m: float = .05
    track_contact_point: bool = False      # reference applies to the ground contact point, not the wheel centre
    slip_threshold_m_s: float = .02        # surface speed that counts as sliding for the friction estimate
    friction_recovery_s: float = 1.5       # how fast that estimate relaxes back once sliding stops
    heading_gain: float = 0.               # N m of yaw reaction per rad of heading error; 0 leaves heading free
    heading_damping: float = .06           # N m s/rad on yaw rate
    heading_torque_limit_Nm: float = .15   # bound on the idle shell's heading torque
    heading_min_lever: float = .12         # skip the hold when the shell axis is this close to horizontal
    unload_fraction: float = .3            # below this share of weight, feedback torque fades out
    airborne_damping_Nms: float = .01      # relative-speed damping while unloaded
    max_lean_deg: float = 28.              # bound on the reference lean
    lqr_weights: str = 'flat'              # gain library: 'flat' (as designed) or 'yaw_aware' (see LQR_WEIGHTS)
    heading_reference: str = 'none'        # heading target: 'none' (start heading if heading_gain > 0), 'start' or 'fall_line'
    heading_actuation: str = 'shells'      # 'shells': unloaded-shell reaction torque; 'lqr': error into the LQR yaw state
    heading_roll_budget_Nm: float = 0.     # > 0 caps the heading torque's horizontal (roll) reaction at this size
    fall_line_slope_deg: tuple = (6., 12.)  # sensed slope over which fall-line alignment fades in
    heading_normal_time_constant_s: float = .5  # low-pass on the normal that sets the fall line
    heading_error_limit_rad: float = .5    # bound on the heading error used
    fall_line_hysteresis_deg: float = 20.  # keep the chosen end of the fall line until the other is this much closer

    def __post_init__(self):
        if self.mode not in CONTROLLER_MODES:
            raise ValueError(f'Choose a controller mode from {CONTROLLER_MODES}')
        if self.shaft_error not in ('material', 'free', 'rolling'):
            raise ValueError('Shaft error must be material, free or rolling')
        if self.rolling_allocation not in ('least_squares', 'damped', 'bounded', 'feasible'):
            raise ValueError('Rolling allocation must be least_squares, damped, bounded or feasible')
        AllocationSettings(self.rolling_speed_limits, self.rolling_acceleration_limits,
                           self.rolling_effort_m_s, self.rolling_change_m_s,
                           self.rolling_weak_effort_m_s if self.rolling_adaptive_effort or self.rolling_allocation == 'feasible' else None,
                           self.rolling_authority_m_s)
        FeasibleReferenceSettings(self.rolling_reference_catchup_gain_s_inv,
                                 self.rolling_reference_catchup_speed_m_s,
                                 self.rolling_reference_position_limit_m)
        if not np.isfinite(self.rolling_damping_m_s) or self.rolling_damping_m_s <= 0:
            raise ValueError('Rolling damping must be finite and positive')
        if self.normal_source not in ('intrinsic', 'solver'):
            raise ValueError('Normal source must be intrinsic or solver')
        if self.mode in OBSERVER_MODES and self.normal_source != 'intrinsic':
            raise ValueError(f'{self.mode} reads no solved contact force; normal_source must be intrinsic')
        if not 0 < self.support_min_load_fraction < 1:
            raise ValueError('support_min_load_fraction must lie between 0 and 1')
        if not self.observer_cutoff_rad_s > 0:
            raise ValueError('observer_cutoff_rad_s must be positive')
        if self.lqr_weights not in LQR_WEIGHTS:
            raise ValueError(f'LQR weights must be one of {sorted(LQR_WEIGHTS)}')
        if self.heading_reference not in ('none', 'start', 'fall_line'):
            raise ValueError('Heading reference must be none, start or fall_line')
        if self.heading_actuation not in ('shells', 'lqr'):
            raise ValueError('Heading actuation must be shells or lqr')
        if self.heading_reference != 'none' and self.heading_actuation == 'shells' and self.heading_gain <= 0:
            raise ValueError('A heading reference acting through the shells needs heading_gain > 0')
        if self.heading_roll_budget_Nm < 0:
            raise ValueError('heading_roll_budget_Nm must be non-negative')
        low, high = self.fall_line_slope_deg
        if not 0 <= low < high:
            raise ValueError('fall_line_slope_deg must be an increasing pair of non-negative angles')
        self.fall_line_slope_deg = (float(low), float(high))

    @property
    def ground_aware(self):
        """True for the modes that use the sensed ground (all but flat_baseline)."""
        return self.mode != 'flat_baseline'

    def as_dict(self):
        return asdict(self)


class FlatDesign:
    """The flat-floor gain library and local designs, built once and shared.

    Uses a private flat ``Plant`` for design, exactly as the interactive lab
    does, so the terrain plant is never perturbed by a design calculation.
    """

    def __init__(self, designer, cache_path=None, weights='flat'):
        if weights not in LQR_WEIGHTS:
            raise ValueError(f'Unknown LQR weights {weights!r}; choose from {sorted(LQR_WEIGHTS)}')
        self.designer, self.cache_path, self.weights = designer, cache_path, weights
        self._local, self._variants = {}, {}
        if LQR_WEIGHTS[weights] is None:
            self.points, self.metadata = build_gain_library(designer, None, cache_path)
        else:
            path = None if cache_path is None else _variant_cache_path(cache_path, weights)
            self.points, self.metadata = build_gain_library(designer, self._design, path)
            self.metadata = {**self.metadata, 'lqr_weights': weights,
                             'weight_overrides': {str(k): v for k, v in LQR_WEIGHTS[weights].items()}}

    def _lqr(self, equilibrium):
        overrides = LQR_WEIGHTS[self.weights]
        if overrides is None:
            return design_lqr(self.designer, equilibrium)
        return design_weighted_lqr(self.designer, equilibrium, overrides)

    def _design(self, phase):
        equilibrium = find_equilibrium(self.designer, phase)
        return {'equilibrium': equilibrium, 'design': self._lqr(equilibrium)}

    def variant(self, weights):
        """The same design with another weight set (built once, then cached)."""
        if weights == self.weights:
            return self
        if weights not in self._variants:
            self._variants[weights] = FlatDesign(self.designer, self.cache_path, weights)
        return self._variants[weights]

    def local(self, phase):
        key = round(float(phase), 6)
        if key not in self._local:
            equilibrium = find_equilibrium(self.designer, phase)
            design = self._lqr(equilibrium)
            if design['full_closed_spectral_radius'] > 1.0001:
                raise RuntimeError('Local feedback has a growing full-state mode')
            self._local[key] = {'phase': float(phase), 'equilibrium': equilibrium, 'design': design}
        return self._local[key]


def _quat_from_rotation_vector(vector):
    angle = float(np.linalg.norm(vector))
    if angle < 1e-12:
        return np.array([1., 0., 0., 0.])
    return np.r_[np.cos(angle/2), np.sin(angle/2)*np.asarray(vector)/angle]


class OffroadController:
    """Five drive commands from the plant state and the measured ground reaction.

    ``command(xy, velocity, acceleration)`` returns ``(applied, diagnostics)``
    where ``applied`` is already inside the actuator bounds. Call
    ``after_step()`` once the plant has advanced. ``reset(ordinary)`` selects
    the ordinary operating point whose equilibrium and shaft-row gain anchor
    the schedule, as the lab's scenario reset does.
    """

    def __init__(self, plant, design, params=None, phase=None, record_diagnostics=False):
        self.params = params or OffroadParams()
        self.record_diagnostics = bool(record_diagnostics)
        if self.record_diagnostics and self.params.mode != 'hardware':
            raise ValueError('Detailed contact diagnostics require hardware mode')
        if self.params.ground_aware and self.params.lqr_weights != getattr(design, 'weights', 'flat'):
            design = design.variant(self.params.lqr_weights)
        self.plant, self.design = plant, design
        self.feedback = ScheduledBalanceFeedback(plant, design.points)
        self.synchronizer = ContactSynchronizer(plant)
        allocator_class = FeasibleRollingAllocator if self.params.rolling_allocation == 'feasible' else BoundedRollingAllocator
        self.rolling_allocator = (allocator_class(AllocationSettings(
            self.params.rolling_speed_limits, self.params.rolling_acceleration_limits,
            self.params.rolling_effort_m_s, self.params.rolling_change_m_s,
            self.params.rolling_weak_effort_m_s if self.params.rolling_adaptive_effort or self.params.rolling_allocation == 'feasible' else None,
            self.params.rolling_authority_m_s))
            if self.params.rolling_allocation in ('bounded', 'feasible') else None)
        self.motion_reference = (FeasibleMotionReference(FeasibleReferenceSettings(
            self.params.rolling_reference_catchup_gain_s_inv,
            self.params.rolling_reference_catchup_speed_m_s,
            self.params.rolling_reference_position_limit_m))
            if self.params.rolling_allocation == 'feasible' else None)
        model = plant.model
        self.carrier_id = mujoco.mj_name2id(model, mujoco.mjtObj.mjOBJ_BODY, 'carrier')
        self.radius = float(plant.config['geometry']['sphere_radius_m'])
        self.gap_half = float(plant.config['geometry'].get('equatorial_gap_half_m', 0.))
        self.gravity = abs(float(plant.config['simulation']['gravity_m_s2'][2]))
        self.total_mass = float(model.body_subtreemass[plant.fork_id])
        self.weight = self.total_mass*self.gravity
        # Lean per unit of contact offset: total weight acting at the wheel
        # radius against the upper body's first moment about the wheel centre.
        upper_moment = float(model.body_mass[plant.fork_id]*model.body_ipos[plant.fork_id][2])
        self.lean_factor = self.total_mass*self.radius/upper_moment
        self.nominal_friction = float(plant.config['contact']['friction'][0])
        # The observer supplies the same force and moment the intrinsic estimate reads.
        self.observer = (MomentumObserver(model, plant.fork_id, self.params.observer_cutoff_rad_s)
                         if self.params.mode in OBSERVER_MODES else None)
        # hardware: the observer's force and moment also say where the support is.
        self.locator = (SupportLocator(model, plant.fork_id, self.radius, self.nominal_friction,
                                       self.params.support_min_load_fraction*self.weight)
                        if self.params.mode == 'hardware' else None)
        self.estimator = ContactEstimator(self.radius, self.weight, self.nominal_friction,
                                          self.params.normal_time_constant_s,
                                          slip_threshold_m_s=self.params.slip_threshold_m_s,
                                          friction_recovery_s=self.params.friction_recovery_s,
                                          source='solver' if self.params.normal_source == 'solver' else 'intrinsic',
                                          rim_time_constant_s=self.params.rim_time_constant_s)
        self.reset(phase)

    def reset(self, phase=None):
        phase = self.plant.config['simulation'].get('initial_shaft_phase_rad', .30) if phase is None else phase
        self.ordinary = self.design.local(phase)
        self.nominal_joints = self.ordinary['equilibrium']['qpos'][7:10].copy()
        if self.observer is not None:
            # No ground truth: start from the static wrench of the current state.
            data = self.plant.data
            self.observer.reset(data.qpos, data.qvel, data.time, self.plant.last_input)
            start = intrinsic_normal(*self.observer.wrench(), self.radius)
            self.estimator.reset(start if start is not None else (0., 0., 1.))
            centre = self.plant.data.xpos[self.plant.fork_id]
            self._plane = (centre-self.radius*self.estimator.normal, self.estimator.normal.copy())
        else:
            ground = getattr(self.plant, 'ground', None)
            self.estimator.reset(ground['normal'] if ground else (0., 0., 1.))
        self._vref = np.zeros(self.plant.model.nv)
        if self.rolling_allocator is not None:
            self.rolling_allocator.reset()
        self._sync = {'commands': [0.]*5, 'blend_weights': [0.]*5, 'targets': []}
        self._held = None
        quat = self.plant.data.qpos[3:7]
        self.heading_reference = float(np.arctan2(2*(quat[0]*quat[3]+quat[1]*quat[2]),
                                                  1-2*(quat[2]**2+quat[3]**2)))
        self._slow_normal = self.estimator.normal.copy()
        self._fall_end = None
        self._yaw_error = None

    def _update_heading_target(self, normal, yaw):
        """Heading error to steer on, or ``None`` when no heading reference is set.

        The error is used by the shell steering in ``_heading_torque`` or, with
        ``heading_actuation='lqr'``, given to the LQR's yaw state.

        'start' holds the heading the run began with. 'fall_line' turns the
        shaft-driven direction (fork x) onto the fall line of the sensed
        ground. The wheel holds a grade along that direction with the shaft,
        whose bound is 43 deg; across it a shell holds 16.7 deg and a pole
        less, so keeping the slope on the shaft keeps it on the strong drive.
        Either end of the fall line will do; the nearer one is kept, with
        hysteresis so a misalignment near 90 deg does not flip the choice
        every interval. Alignment fades in with slope and is off on level
        ground, where heading does not matter and only yaw rate is damped.
        The normal is filtered more slowly than the balance normal so that the
        target does not jump at every DEM facet.
        """
        params, dt = self.params, self.plant.dt
        blend = dt/(params.heading_normal_time_constant_s+dt)
        mixed = self._slow_normal+blend*(np.asarray(normal)-self._slow_normal)
        self._slow_normal = mixed/np.linalg.norm(mixed)
        if params.heading_reference == 'none':
            return None
        if params.heading_reference == 'start':
            error = (yaw-self.heading_reference+np.pi) % (2*np.pi)-np.pi
            return float(np.clip(error, -params.heading_error_limit_rad, params.heading_error_limit_rad))
        n = self._slow_normal
        slope = float(np.degrees(np.arccos(np.clip(n[2], -1., 1.))))
        low, high = params.fall_line_slope_deg
        weight = float(np.clip((slope-low)/(high-low), 0., 1.))
        if weight <= 0.:
            return 0.
        fall = float(np.arctan2(n[1], n[0]))

        def miss(end):
            return abs((yaw-fall-end+np.pi) % (2*np.pi)-np.pi)
        nearer = min((0., np.pi), key=miss)
        if self._fall_end is None or miss(self._fall_end) > miss(nearer)+np.radians(params.fall_line_hysteresis_deg):
            self._fall_end = nearer
        error = (yaw-fall-self._fall_end+np.pi) % (2*np.pi)-np.pi
        return weight*float(np.clip(error, -params.heading_error_limit_rad, params.heading_error_limit_rad))

    def _rim_support(self, loads):
        """``(axis, hold)`` while the support point lies in the equatorial gap, else ``(None, False)``.

        There the wheel rides on one or both shell rims, up to the half gap
        away from where a sphere would touch. The carrier angle against the
        current normal estimate locates the support, so this needs no extra
        sensing. ``gap_margin_m`` widens the band a little for the estimate's
        own lag. ``axis`` is the carrier's x axis, along which a rim displaces
        the contact. ``hold`` is true when the wheel is resting there on both
        rims, moving slower than ``rim_hold_speed_m_s``.
        """
        axis = self.plant.data.xmat[self.carrier_id].reshape(3, 3)[:, 0].copy()
        offset = self.radius*abs(float(axis@self.estimator.normal))
        if offset > self.gap_half+self.params.gap_margin_m:
            return None, False
        total = sum(loads.values())
        both = total > 0 and min(loads['shell_A'], loads['shell_B']) > .05*total
        resting = float(np.linalg.norm(self.plant.data.qvel[:2])) < self.params.rim_hold_speed_m_s
        return axis, bool(both and resting)

    # -- reference ----------------------------------------------------------
    def lean_vector(self, normal, acceleration):
        """Horizontal part of the desired body-up direction."""
        lean = np.asarray(acceleration, float)/self.gravity
        if self.params.ground_aware:
            lean = lean-self.params.lean_gain*self.lean_factor*np.asarray(normal)[:2]
        limit = np.sin(np.deg2rad(self.params.max_lean_deg))
        size = float(np.linalg.norm(lean))
        return lean*limit/size if size > limit else lean

    def reference_attitude(self, normal, acceleration):
        """Reference quaternion before heading is applied."""
        lean = self.lean_vector(normal, acceleration)
        size = float(np.linalg.norm(lean))
        vector = np.zeros(3) if size < 1e-12 else np.arcsin(size)*np.array([-lean[1], lean[0], 0.])/size
        quat = np.empty(4)
        mujoco.mju_mulQuat(quat, self.ordinary['equilibrium']['qpos'][3:7], _quat_from_rotation_vector(vector))
        return quat

    def support_torque(self, normal, loads):
        """Static shaft and shell torques that carry the weight through the sensed contact point."""
        data = self.plant.data
        shaft_axis = data.xmat[self.plant.fork_id].reshape(3, 3)[:, 1]
        shell_axis = data.xmat[self.carrier_id].reshape(3, 3)[:, 0]
        total = sum(loads.values())
        lever = self.weight*self.radius
        torque = np.zeros(5)
        torque[0] = lever*np.cross(shaft_axis, normal)[2]
        if total > 1e-6:
            shell = lever*np.cross(shell_axis, normal)[2]
            torque[1] = shell*loads['shell_A']/total
            torque[2] = shell*loads['shell_B']/total
        return torque

    # -- command --------------------------------------------------------------
    def command(self, xy, velocity, acceleration):
        plant, params = self.plant, self.params
        equilibrium, design = self.ordinary['equilibrium'], self.ordinary['design']
        records = None
        if self.locator is not None:
            # Nothing solved is read. The observer's force and moment locate the
            # support, and the records built from them stand in for the contacts.
            data = plant.data
            self.observer.update(data.qpos, data.qvel, plant.last_input, data.time)
            sensed = self.observer.measurement(self.radius)
            records = self.locator.records(data, *self.observer.wrench())
            measurement = measure_records(plant, records)
            # The surface's speed over the ground at the located point: the
            # mismatch between measured motion and rolling, i.e. the slip.
            sensed.update(slip_velocity_m_s=measurement['slip_velocity_m_s'],
                          slip_speed_m_s=measurement['slip_speed_m_s'])
        else:
            measurement = measure_contact(plant)
            sensed = measurement
            if self.observer is not None:
                data = plant.data
                self.observer.update(data.qpos, data.qvel, plant.last_input, data.time)
                sensed = self.observer.measurement(self.radius)
        rim = self._rim_support(measurement['loads_N']) if params.rim_aware else (None, False)
        estimate = self.estimator.update(sensed, plant.dt, *rim)
        aware = params.ground_aware
        normal = estimate['normal'] if aware else np.array([0., 0., 1.])
        if aware:
            quat = plant.data.qpos[3:7]
            yaw = float(np.arctan2(2*(quat[0]*quat[3]+quat[1]*quat[2]), 1-2*(quat[2]**2+quat[3]**2)))
            self._yaw_error = self._update_heading_target(normal, yaw)
        moving = bool(np.linalg.norm(velocity) > 0)
        touching = sum(measurement['loads_N'].values()) > .001
        nominal_center = np.asarray(xy, float).copy()
        if aware and params.track_contact_point:
            nominal_center += self.radius*normal[:2]
        reference_acceleration = np.asarray(acceleration, float)
        relaxed_position_error = np.zeros(2)
        damping = params.rolling_damping_m_s if params.rolling_allocation == 'damped' else 0.
        if self.rolling_allocator is not None:
            if (moving or self.motion_reference is not None) and aware and not touching and self._held is not None:
                # Keep the last bounded references through a brief dropout.
                # Their change is zero, so both reference bounds still hold.
                drive = self.rolling_allocator.hold()
                body_velocity = (self.rolling_allocator.diagnostics['allocation_accepted_velocity_m_s']
                                 if self.motion_reference is not None else velocity)
                vref = np.r_[body_velocity, 0., 0., 0., 0., drive[:3]]
                feedforward = np.r_[np.zeros(3), drive[3:]]
            else:
                contact_records = getattr(plant, 'contact_records', None)
                bounded_records = (records if records is not None else
                                   contact_records() if contact_records is not None else records_from_mujoco(plant))
                vref, feedforward = rolling_feedforward_records(
                    plant, bounded_records, np.asarray(velocity, float), allocator=self.rolling_allocator,
                    reference=self.motion_reference, position=nominal_center)
        elif not moving:
            vref, feedforward = np.zeros(9), np.zeros(5)
        elif aware and not touching and self._held is not None:
            # A surface handover can leave one interval with no contact. The
            # rolling allocation needs a contact, so keep the last one rather
            # than drop the velocity reference to zero for that interval.
            vref, feedforward = self._held['vref'].copy(), self._held['feedforward'].copy()
        elif records is not None:
            vref, feedforward = rolling_feedforward_records(plant, records, np.asarray(velocity, float), damping)
        else:
            vref, feedforward = rolling_feedforward(plant, np.asarray(velocity, float), damping)
        qref = equilibrium['qpos'].copy()
        qref[:2] = xy
        if self.motion_reference is not None:
            allocated = self.rolling_allocator.diagnostics
            reference = self.motion_reference.feedback(
                nominal_center, plant.data.qpos[:2], acceleration,
                allocated['allocation_accepted_velocity_m_s'], allocated['allocation_velocity_response'],
                valid=allocated['allocation_accepted_velocity_valid'])
            qref[:2] = reference['xy']
            vref[:2] = reference['velocity']
            reference_acceleration = reference['acceleration']
            relaxed_position_error = reference['relaxed_position_error_m']
        elif aware and params.track_contact_point:
            # The reference names where the wheel should touch the ground. The
            # centre sits one radius along the ground normal from that point,
            # so on a slope it is offset horizontally and need not be dragged
            # back over the reference as the slope comes and goes.
            qref[:2] = np.asarray(xy)+self.radius*normal[:2]
        qref[7:10] = self.nominal_joints
        qref[3:7] = self.reference_attitude(normal, reference_acceleration)
        diagnostics = {'measurement': measurement, 'estimate': estimate, 'drive_fade': 1.,
                       'observed': sensed if self.observer is not None else None,
                       'support_torque': np.zeros(5), 'reference_quat': qref[3:7].copy(),
                       'heading_target_error_rad': self._yaw_error if aware else None}
        if aware:
            command, feedback = self._terrain_feedback(qref, vref, feedforward, normal, measurement, touching)
            diagnostics['support_torque'] = feedback.pop('support_torque')
        else:
            command, feedback = self.feedback.compute(qref, vref, equilibrium['u'], design['K'], feedforward)
        diagnostics.update(effective_phase_rad=feedback['effective_phase_rad'], yaw_rad=feedback['yaw_rad'],
                           error=feedback['error'])
        if self.record_diagnostics:
            pre_sync = command.copy()
            diagnostics.update(equilibrium_command=np.array(equilibrium['u'], copy=True),
                               rolling_feedforward=np.array(feedforward, copy=True),
                               support_command=diagnostics['support_torque'].copy(),
                               lqr_command=-feedback['K']@feedback['error'],
                               feedback_reference_qvel=feedback['reference_qvel'].copy(),
                               feedback_gain=np.array(feedback['K'], copy=True))
        sync = {'commands': [0.]*5, 'blend_weights': [0.]*5, 'targets': []}
        if params.synchronize and records is not None:
            # The plane through the located support with the sensed normal,
            # kept from the last loaded interval while the wheel is unloaded.
            if records:
                centre = plant.data.xpos[plant.fork_id]
                self._plane = (centre-self.radius*estimate['normal'], estimate['normal'].copy())
            sync = self.synchronizer.preview(command, records=records, ground=self._plane)
            weights = np.asarray(sync['blend_weights'])
            command = (1.-weights)*command+weights*np.asarray(sync['commands'])
        elif params.synchronize:
            # The preview owns a private MjData; give it the current ground plane.
            self.synchronizer._data.mocap_pos[:] = plant.data.mocap_pos
            self.synchronizer._data.mocap_quat[:] = plant.data.mocap_quat
            sync = self.synchronizer.preview(command)
            weights = np.asarray(sync['blend_weights'])
            command = (1.-weights)*command+weights*np.asarray(sync['commands'])
        if self.record_diagnostics:
            diagnostics['transition_command_delta'] = command-pre_sync
            pre_heading = command.copy()
        if aware:
            heading_torque, diagnostics['heading_error_rad'] = self._heading_torque(feedback['yaw_rad'])
            command = command+heading_torque
            if self.record_diagnostics:
                diagnostics['heading_command'] = command-pre_heading
                pre_fade = command.copy()
            command, diagnostics['drive_fade'] = self._fade_when_unloaded(command, estimate, vref)
            if self.record_diagnostics:
                diagnostics['fade_command_delta'] = command-pre_fade
        self._sync, self._vref = sync, vref
        applied = np.clip(command, -plant.bounds, plant.bounds)
        diagnostics.update(requested=command.copy(), reference_qpos=qref, reference_qvel=vref,
                           sync_weights=np.asarray(sync['blend_weights']))
        if self.record_diagnostics:
            defaults = dict(
                allocation_requested_velocity_m_s=np.asarray(velocity, float).copy(),
                allocation_accepted_velocity_m_s=vref[:2].copy(),
                allocation_velocity_response=np.eye(2),
                allocation_modal_effort_m_s=np.full(2, np.nan),
                allocation_accepted_velocity_valid=False,
                allocation_reference_residual_rms_m_s=np.nan)
            if self.rolling_allocator is not None:
                defaults.update(self.rolling_allocator.diagnostics)
            diagnostics.update(defaults)
            diagnostics.update(feedback_reference_xy=qref[:2].copy(),
                reference_acceleration_m_s2=reference_acceleration.copy(),
                reference_position_error_relaxed_m=relaxed_position_error.copy(),
                reference_catchup_velocity_m_s=defaults['allocation_requested_velocity_m_s']-np.asarray(velocity, float))
            from .contact_kinematics import rolling_consistency
            diagnostics.update(rolling_consistency(plant, records,
                self.params.support_min_load_fraction*self.weight))
            diagnostics.update(
                estimated_contact_point_m=np.asarray(measurement['point_m']).copy(),
                estimated_support_loads_N=np.array([measurement['loads_N'][name]
                    for name in ('shell_A', 'shell_B', 'belt_A', 'belt_B')]),
                reference_velocity_m_s=np.asarray(velocity, float).copy(),
                tracking_velocity_error_m_s=plant.data.qvel[:2]-np.asarray(velocity, float),
                actuator_saturated=np.abs(command) > plant.bounds+1e-12,
                clipping_command_delta=applied-command,
                input_bounds=plant.bounds.copy())
            if self.rolling_allocator is not None:
                diagnostics.update(self.rolling_allocator.diagnostics)
            else:
                diagnostics.update(
                    allocation_drive_reference=np.r_[vref[6:9], feedforward[3:]],
                    allocation_speed_limits=np.full(5, np.nan),
                    allocation_acceleration_limits=np.full(5, np.nan),
                    allocation_active_bounds=np.zeros(5, bool),
                    allocation_effort_weight_m_s=damping,
                    allocation_change_weight_m_s=0.,
                    allocation_authority_m_s=np.nan,
                    allocation_effort_blend=0.,
                    allocation_residual_rms_m_s=np.nan,
                    allocation_velocity_error_m_s=np.full(2, np.nan))
        return applied, diagnostics

    def _terrain_feedback(self, qref, vref, feedforward, normal, measurement, touching):
        plant, params = self.plant, self.params
        equilibrium, design = self.ordinary['equilibrium'], self.ordinary['design']
        rotation = plant.data.xmat[self.carrier_id].reshape(3, 3)
        support = rotation.T@(normal if params.schedule_on_ground_normal else np.array([0., 0., 1.]))
        phase = float(np.arctan2(-support[0], support[2]))
        if touching:
            loads = dict(measurement['loads_N'])
            gain = self.feedback._gain(phase, loads)
            self._held = {'loads': loads, 'gain': gain.copy(), 'vref': np.array(vref, copy=True),
                          'feedforward': np.array(feedforward, copy=True)}
        elif self._held is not None:
            # Unloaded for an interval: the surface that was carrying the wheel
            # is still the one under it. Falling back to the ordinary-pose gain
            # here would apply, say, shell gains while a belt is underneath.
            loads, gain = self._held['loads'], self._held['gain'].copy()
        else:
            loads, gain = dict(measurement['loads_N']), np.array(design['K'], copy=True)
        gain[0] = design['K'][0]
        quat = plant.data.qpos[3:7]
        yaw = float(np.arctan2(2*(quat[0]*quat[3]+quat[1]*quat[2]), 1-2*(quat[2]**2+quat[3]**2)))
        c, s = np.cos(yaw), np.sin(yaw)
        world_to_heading = np.array([[c, s], [-s, c]])
        target = np.array(qref, copy=True)
        mujoco.mju_mulQuat(target[3:7], np.array(qref[3:7]), np.array([np.cos(yaw/2), 0., 0., np.sin(yaw/2)]))
        # Follow the ground: its height and the vertical speed of travelling
        # along it are not errors, and no drive can act on them anyway.
        target[2] = plant.data.qpos[2]
        vref = np.array(vref, copy=True)
        vref[2] = -float(normal[:2]@vref[:2])/max(normal[2], .2)
        error = plant.error(target, vref)
        error[:2] = world_to_heading@error[:2]
        error[9:11] = world_to_heading@error[9:11]
        error[6:9] = (error[6:9]+np.pi) % (2*np.pi)-np.pi
        error[7:9] = 0.
        if params.shaft_error == 'free':
            error[6] = 0.
        elif params.shaft_error == 'rolling':
            # Rolling without slip ties x, pitch and shaft angle together:
            # x - R (pitch + shaft) is constant. Ground travelled along an
            # undulating surface breaks the flat bookkeeping of shaft angle, so
            # take the shaft error that is consistent with the position error.
            error[6] = error[0]/self.radius-error[4]
        if self._yaw_error is not None and params.heading_actuation == 'lqr':
            # The target above carries the current yaw, so error[5] is zero
            # and heading is free. A heading reference replaces it.
            error[5] = self._yaw_error
        support_torque = params.support_feedforward*self.support_torque(normal, loads)
        command = np.asarray(equilibrium['u'])+np.asarray(feedforward)+support_torque-gain@error
        return command, {'effective_phase_rad': phase, 'yaw_rad': yaw, 'error': error,
                         'support_torque': support_torque, 'K': gain, 'reference_qvel': vref}

    def _heading_torque(self, yaw):
        """Hold the starting heading with whichever shell is not carrying load.

        A shell that is off the ground is a reaction wheel about the carrier's
        x axis. That axis is tilted out of the horizontal by the carrier phase,
        so the unloaded shell's motor reaction has a vertical component, and
        that is the only yaw authority a single point contact leaves. Rolling
        sideways spins the loaded shell about the same tilted axis and turns
        the platform the other way; counter-spinning the idle shell cancels
        it. Nothing is available when the axis is near horizontal, so the hold
        is skipped there, and when both shells are loaded.

        The reaction's horizontal part is a roll disturbance that the balance
        feedback has to absorb, which is why the torque is kept small.

        With a heading reference acting through the shells, the target is that
        reference ('start' or 'fall_line') instead of the starting heading.
        ``heading_roll_budget_Nm`` then bounds the horizontal part of the
        reaction rather than the torque: near a pole, where the support is on
        a belt and both shell axes are close to vertical, the reaction is
        almost pure yaw and the full torque limit is used; on a shell, where the
        axis is close to horizontal, the same budget allows only a small
        torque. Heading is then steered mostly during pole crossings, which
        shaft-driven travel makes twice per wheel revolution.
        """
        params, plant = self.params, self.plant
        error = float((yaw-self.heading_reference+np.pi) % (2*np.pi)-np.pi)
        torque = np.zeros(5)
        steering = params.heading_actuation == 'shells' and self._yaw_error is not None
        if params.heading_gain <= 0 or self._held is None:
            return torque, error
        if steering:
            error = self._yaw_error
        elif params.heading_reference != 'none':
            return torque, error
        loads = self._held['loads']
        total = sum(loads.values())
        free = [index for index, name in ((1, 'shell_A'), (2, 'shell_B')) if loads[name] <= .05*total]
        vertical = float(plant.data.xmat[self.carrier_id].reshape(3, 3)[2, 0])
        if not free or abs(vertical) < params.heading_min_lever:
            return torque, error
        velocity = np.empty(6)
        mujoco.mj_objectVelocity(plant.model, plant.data, mujoco.mjtObj.mjOBJ_BODY, plant.fork_id, velocity, 0)
        wanted = -(params.heading_gain*error+params.heading_damping*velocity[2])
        limit = params.heading_torque_limit_Nm
        if params.heading_roll_budget_Nm > 0:
            horizontal = float(np.sqrt(max(1.-vertical*vertical, 0.)))
            limit = min(limit, params.heading_roll_budget_Nm/max(horizontal, 1e-6))
        # Motor torque t on a free shell reacts on the platform as -t * axis.
        each = float(np.clip(-wanted/vertical, -limit, limit))
        torque[free] = each/len(free)
        return torque, error

    def _fade_when_unloaded(self, command, estimate, vref):
        """Stop driving the wheel while it carries little or no load.

        Off the ground a torque only spins its drive up, and the wheel then
        lands sliding. Below ``unload_fraction`` of weight the torques fade and
        the drives are damped toward their rolling reference instead. The
        filtered load is used, so a single-interval dropout at a surface
        handover does not trigger it.
        """
        params, plant = self.params, self.plant
        fade = float(np.clip(estimate['load_fraction']/max(params.unload_fraction, 1e-6), 0., 1.))
        if fade >= 1.:
            return command, 1.
        command = np.array(command, copy=True)
        damping = -params.airborne_damping_Nms*(plant.data.qvel[6:9]-vref[6:9])
        command[:3] = fade*command[:3]+(1.-fade)*damping
        return command, fade

    def after_step(self):
        """Advance the material joint references, as the lab runtime does."""
        dt = self.plant.dt
        self.nominal_joints += dt*self._vref[6:9]
        for target in self._sync['targets']:
            if 'target_joint_speed_rad_s' in target:
                index = target['command_index']
                self.nominal_joints[index] += dt*target['blend_weight']*target['target_joint_speed_rad_s']
