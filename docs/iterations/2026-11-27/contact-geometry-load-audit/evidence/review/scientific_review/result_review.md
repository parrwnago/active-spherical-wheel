# I-66 scientific result review

This note interprets the frozen canonical audit and its separately registered structural-routing supplement. It adds no counterfactual, fit, threshold, physics run or independent arithmetic experiment.

## Geometry contributes, but is not the complete explanation

On the 41 I-65 lost-useful frames, replacing only the correct-family candidate point by the sampled family centroid lowers vector RMS from **0.139264 to 0.105682 N m**; 37 frames improve and four worsen. The registered geometric-improvement qualification nevertheless fails: the -1 rad phase has one focus frame, at 32.110 s, whose error increases. This failed uniform-phase criterion does not erase the aggregate geometric contribution.

The frozen truth-assisted ranking counterfactual corrects 27 of those 41 lost decisions. Across all useful cases, correct preferences increase **253→276/294**, including four previously correct decisions becoming incorrect. Harmful cases improve **2→4/6**. Only the 300 distinct-family useful/harmful opportunities are scored; duplicate same-family comparisons are not artificially separated. The sampled point and correct branch are supplied by evaluation truth, so this is not a deployable estimator or live-controller result.

## The fixed events show different mechanisms

At **35.205 s, -1 rad**, the physically valid candidate in the correct native family (belt B) is 26.036 mm from the sampled centroid. Replacing that native alternative's point lowers its raw residual norm **0.42181→0.20759 N m**. Its frozen covariance score falls from about 137.81 to 43.08, below the unchanged wrong proposal's 132.65: the old example is corrected. This distance belongs to the physical Nalt candidate, not the original native equivalent point or the wrong-family F point.

At **32.110 s, -1 rad**, the correct proposal's point is only 2.789 mm from the centroid, yet replacement worsens the norm **0.11272→0.16406 N m** and does not repair the ranking. The full-wrench representation gives 0.01621 N m. At **6.850 s, +0.9 rad**, replacement instead gives a large improvement **0.26861→0.01223 N m** and repairs the useful decision. The old good 7.875 s example worsens **0.09879→0.15850 N m**, while remaining correctly preferred. A single geometric story does not explain all fixed examples.

The two harmful cases still misranked after point replacement are **31.095 s and 31.175 s, +0.9 rad**. Correct-alternative scores remain above the wrong proposal's (36.04 versus 19.55; 4.717 versus 3.597). Their sampled-point residual norms are 0.17881 and 0.07986 N m; full-wrench residuals are 0.05276 and 0.00747 N m. These remaining discrepancies are mainly in active joint rows/free-couple terms, not predominantly the inactive-joint floor. The full-wrench model was not rescored or recalibrated, so these values do not establish that a wrench-based classifier would fix either decision.

## Structural floor: exact statement and limits

In the mirrored model, a joint outside a family's ancestor chain has zero Jacobian column for any force point or full wrench assigned to that family. Its residual therefore equals the recorded raw generalized joint load regardless of point choice. The registered supplementary audit confirms the inactive-row identities exactly.

Its floor RMS is 0.039036 N m for the 41 focus frames, representing 13.64% of sampled-reference squared residual; for the six harmful cases it is 0.135178 N m and 72.68%. The stable correct-pair subgroup has a much smaller 0.002128 N m floor and 1.21% share. These are lower-bound shares of a coordinate error metric, not causal percentages or independently measured force errors.

At 35.205 s the shell-B residual **-0.206627 N m** is unchanged under candidate, sampled-point and full-wrench models because the true belt-B body's shell-B Jacobian row is zero. That floor is below the unchanged approximately 0.25106 N m component bound; it does not prove that the old compatibility check or ranking must fail. In fact, point replacement fixes that ranking. The largest inactive-floor examples must not be used to explain the two different harmful cases that remain wrong.

A nonzero inactive load shows that the recorded interval load cannot be fully represented by one wrench on that endpoint family in the mirrored model. It does not identify the reason. Contact on another body earlier within the preceding 5 ms, model/actuation/constraint bias, or other observer error remain possible. It is not evidence of CPU computation delay, and current endpoint contact can still be correct.

## Compatibility, wrench and share-proxy caveats

Focus compatibility counts remain **39/41→39/41** despite repaired rankings: one failure becomes a pass, one pass becomes a failure, and one remains a failure. Absolute compatibility and relative covariance preference answer different questions. The geometric lever-effect/error ratio must not be described as a causal fraction of error explained.

The structural supplement records exactly one positive family normal load at each of the 41 focus, 294 useful and six harmful endpoint samples. Consequently the normal-share proxy equals the sampled-point model on those cohorts. This is a degeneracy of this comparison, not validation or refutation of multiple-contact estimation. One positive family may contain several contacts, and an endpoint sample does not reveal the preceding 5 ms history.

The full-wrench model improves focus RMS further to 0.068932 N m and passes the old component bound on all 41 focus frames. It uses force, moment and joint targets from the same observer/model, however. Its extra moment/free-couple term is not independent true-contact evidence; endpoint/midpoint conventions and missing interval force routing still apply.

## Specific next questions, without new acceptance tuning

A future frozen candidate-bank coverage audit can distinguish whether a correct-family centroid lies outside the existing bank or whether the existing ranking selected a poorer point. The 35.205 s alternative motivates that question; it is not performed here. The small-offset, worsening 32.110 s example separately motivates load-representation/interval investigation.

To distinguish actual within-interval routing from model bias, future separately authorized instrumentation would need synchronized substep contact-force/body information over the same observer interval. The current endpoint logs cannot provide it retrospectively. Neither further cutoff reshaping nor an untested broader point search follows automatically from this result.
