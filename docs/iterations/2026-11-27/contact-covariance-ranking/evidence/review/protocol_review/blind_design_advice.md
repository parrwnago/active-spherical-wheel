# I-65 blind design advice

This read-only audit precedes new calibration/scoring on historical arrays. I-60 through I-64 outcomes are already known. It records advice pending the authoritative protocol, not a completed experiment or unseen validation. Live native LQR, momentum observer and feedforward remain selected.

The proposed primary method is one shared residual-vector calibration for raw-interval M1; matched-history M2 is a predeclared control, not an alternative selected after outcomes. Use the same candidate-independent old force scale for both physical current alternatives. One mean and covariance must apply identically to F and Nalt. A Mahalanobis score can weight mismatch directions/correlations that maxabs scoring discarded, but it is empirical residual consistency, not a calibrated posterior or independent force measurement.

The root proposal of rho=0.2 shrinkage toward trace(S)/3 times identity plus0.05 squared identity is defensible as a fixed engineering regularizer in normalized units. It is not measured sensor noise or a motor limit. With equal training-episode weights, compute covariance around the single pooled mean; do not remove each episode's mean before pooling and thereby erase between-episode bias. Recommend at least100 selected timestamps per training episode as a numerical sanity check; they are not100 independent observations. Failed calibration must be explicit and retain A.

Train only on primary accepted-proposal timestamps from the other two entire episodes. Select the physical F branch if its family matches truth; otherwise select the physical native-family alternative if correct. Select at most one per timestamp, never choose by residual magnitude or truth-point distance. When both families are correct, verify the frozen family-best candidates coincide. Correct family does not guarantee correct point or a correct5ms load model. Rows where neither alternative is correct remain in evaluation denominators even though they cannot supply a positive calibration sample.

A normalized predicted-pattern difference at most0.01 and a relative score tie tolerance1e-9 times max(1,sF,sN) are acceptable if explicitly described as fixed engineering/numerical ambiguity, not statistical confidence. Unknown, tied or pattern-ambiguous rows retain A. Prefer computing the pattern contrast directly from the two predicted joint-load vectors; subtracting residuals is algebraically equivalent but can cancel a large common observed term. Near torque-null configurations contain little information; covariance normalization cannot create it. A candidate must not receive its own error-dependent variance or normalize its error by itself.

If M2 receives covariance-aware scores, fit its own shared model using the same training-only rule, since its residual distribution differs from M1. Do not apply M1 calibration to M2 without an explicit transformation. Full covariance does not make the observer's derived force/joint channels independent, and a lower relative score can still mean that both candidates fit poorly. Report this limitation without inventing a new absolute gate after outcomes.

Use whole-episode folds. All three were previously inspected, so holdout folds are retrospective internal assessment; the +0.3 episode has no harmful examples and its relative harmful rate is unscorable. Freeze all representation, teacher, weighting, regularization, sample, ambiguity and tie rules before fitting. Report coverage and full-group preference counts, including unknowns and ambiguities. A promising discrimination result needs at least3/6 harmful and265/294 useful choices, baseline nonregression, and separate unchanged I-62 shadow label/point/phase gates. The pooled errors<=A gate is stronger than90% useful retention: only six harmful changes can be removed, so at most six useful losses are affordable. Ranking promise alone is not acceptance.

A clear N preference may veto accepted F only to the original native-equivalent fallback, never a new point. New vetoes must not alter the frozen histories. A future single calibration fitted across all three episodes would be a different validation stage requiring a fresh native episode; this approved experiment is offline only, with no new physics or automatic live adoption. Failure would limit this particular empirical quadratic ranking, not prove that all nonlinear or condition-dependent estimators are impossible.
