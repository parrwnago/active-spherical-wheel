## What is established

- The frozen usable bank supports the same 27/41 focus repairs as the sampled-reference counterfactual; the other 14 focus rows are repaired by neither.
- Capacity is 280/294 useful and 5/6 harmful. This includes all original successful choices by set inclusion; that nonregression is a QA invariant, not a learned-selector achievement.
- Nearest geometry repairs 25/41 focus cases, while a minimum-score/existence search repairs 27/41. Geometric and load objectives differ.
- The focus median point distance decreases from selected 6.257 mm to nearest-bank 0.813 mm. No arbitrary good-distance threshold is introduced.
- The nearest pre-admission and usable distances agree on focus/harmful cases. This means the nearest projected geometry is usable; it does not prove that every excluded point lacks a lower load score.

At 35.205 s, the selected correct-family alternative is 26.036 mm from the sampled point, while an existing usable point is 4.696 mm away and yields the correct preference. The original fused rule nevertheless correctly selects its own minimum: the velocity-cost term increases from 53.004 to 58.544 at the nearer point and outweighs improvements in other terms. This establishes an objective tradeoff, not an implementation bug or proof of actual slip.

At 6.850 s, +0.9 rad, an existing 0.314 mm point also repairs the ranking, but its frozen velocity term is 7.1614 versus 0.2667 for the selected point. Conversely, at 13.470 s, +0.3 rad, the nearer point has better velocity consistency but worse normal/moment costs, and no existing point repairs the preference. Velocity weighting is therefore not a demonstrated explanation for every failure.

## What is not established

Minimum joint-load score is not true geometry. The harmful cohort's minimum-score point has median 19.50 mm and maximum 33.53 mm discrepancy. At 35.205 s the score-minimizing point is farther from the centroid than the original selection. At 31.175 s, +0.9 rad, a bank-only rank repair uses a point 20.60 mm away while the nearest 0.378 mm point does not win. Such capacity can reflect compensation for load/model bias.

The frozen classifier still cannot repair 31.095 s among existing correct-family candidates, and neither the bank nor sampled reference repairs the first lost-useful 32.110 s example. These limitations remain rather than being relabeled as missing geometric coverage.

The candidate pool is restricted using evaluation truth, the wrong branch is held fixed, and the point/load references remain endpoint samples for an earlier interval. No capacity count is an achievable live family-error rate. The selected live LQR/observer/geometry/feedforward/preparation pipeline remains unchanged.

The evidence favors investigating selection/objective assumptions before enlarging this bank or relaxing admission. It does not justify automatically selecting minimum-Mahalanobis points, retuning velocity weights or implementing load sharing. Residual timing/force-routing assumptions remain unresolved and require separate frozen investigation or appropriately instrumented future data.
