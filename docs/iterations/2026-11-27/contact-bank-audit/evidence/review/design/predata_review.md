Status: no new historical bank coordinates, gaps, scores or capacity outcomes computed. The authoritative protocol is `protocol/protocol.json`.

The protocol appropriately continues the geometry/load investigation using the original 102 slots, candidate validity, motion/force inputs and original fused objective. It adds no deployable selection rule, point search or coefficient tuning. The candidate helper preserves the original seed/projection operation order and keeps pre-admission slots distinct from usable candidates.

## Separate the questions

For the truth-family physical candidate selected by the original process, report:

- Selected and minimum usable point distance to the sampled centroid; their nonnegative difference is a geometric selection gap.
- Minimum pre-admission distance separately; the usable-minus-pre-admission distance gap describes admission's geometric effect without making excluded slots usable.
- Selected-minus-minimum usable I-65 score, and the signed minimum-usable-minus-sampled-reference score. The sampled point is not a guaranteed score or 5 ms physics floor.
- Minimum usable prediction distance to the sampled-reference load, in N m, and cross-metrics at the selected, nearest-point and minimum-score indices.

A force-parallel point change has zero moment-arm effect. Conversely, a low learned consistency score can come from geometric error that cancels model bias. Point distance, load representation and statistical ranking capacity therefore require separate outputs; no universal 1 mm coverage label is justified.

## Capacity is not the minimum-score classifier

A minimum-score candidate can lie within the frozen pattern-ambiguity region. The primary capacity question must test whether **any** usable correct-family candidate produces a correct preference under the unchanged ambiguity and relative-tie rules. Minimum score remains a useful continuous diagnostic, not sufficient proof of a resolved decision.

The fixed success statement is precise: all 27 useful and two harmful I-66 reference-repaired decisions are reproducible by existing usable candidates. If not, report exact supported/unsupported states rather than calling the bank broadly inadequate. A reference-only repair indicates a conditional finite-bank/admission limitation under the current point-load score; neither repair leaves geometry/model/calibration ambiguity. An existing bank-only repair can reflect biased point compensation, not true-point accuracy.

The original correct-family selected candidate must first reproduce as a usable member. Existence-based correct decisions cannot then regress on the 300 useful/harmful opportunities: the original point remains available. This is an inclusion and implementation invariant, not an estimator-improvement gate. A minimum-score-only choice could regress through ambiguity and must not be substituted for the existence result.

## Original selection semantics

The original family best minimizes the **total frozen fused cost**, consisting of angle, moment and velocity terms; it does not select the closest geometric point or independently minimize each term. I-61's physical native alternative uses that same frozen within-native-family fused minimum. Better geometry or a lower later I-65 score elsewhere in the bank does not itself establish an original selection implementation bug.

At the 14 fixed anchors, cross-report each selected/nearest/minimum-score index's unchanged cost components. This can identify objective tradeoffs without retuning weights or claiming why a physical rolling assumption failed. Keep original raw IDs and family assignment when handling duplicate geometry.

Only the 294 useful/six harmful distinct-family pairs support the original wrong-branch-fixed capacity comparison. Event-window states where neither original family is correct retain their geometry context but do not gain an invented correct-versus-wrong pair. All cohorts, ROI indices and events must remain fixed before new arithmetic.

The sampled family mean is a last-step normal-load-weighted centroid and may be interior to the sphere. All load alternatives remain endpoint single-family point-force explanations of an earlier interval load; an audit cannot establish exact contact history, physical tracking improvement or CPU delay. The standalone context document should preserve those distinctions and separate the selected live pipeline from all I-60–I-67 offline diagnostics.
