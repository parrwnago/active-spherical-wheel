`reviewed_before_truth_evaluation_utc` overstate the timing of that final receipt.
The independent evaluator mathematics had already been reviewed in the earlier
pre-truth source discussion, and the independent numerical scripts were frozen
before outcomes. However, the final receipt binding the revised evaluator's
guard checks was written at **06:15:54.844303 UTC**, while the canonical process
registered its impending truth read at **06:15:38.326945 UTC**.

Therefore the final receipt itself is not evidence that canonical truth
evaluation waited for that receipt. At its creation the reviewer had not read
I-61 residual efficacy or gate outcomes. It records a source approval without
outcome knowledge, but its global “before truth evaluation” label is inaccurate.
The original receipt is preserved; this addendum corrects interpretation.

The independent analytic feature driver was sealed at 06:09:57.380526 UTC and
the independent evaluation script at 06:14:31.802499 UTC, both before the
canonical truth-read registration. Root protocol, selected candidate bank,
new inference source and thresholds were also frozen before inference. The
later independent arithmetic matches the final canonical outputs. This
chronology clarification changes no algorithm, result, threshold or gate.
