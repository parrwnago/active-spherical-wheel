# I-81 full closed evidence archive

This captures one unchanged native Gazebo/DART figure-eight prefix through
102 seconds, its registered sources/inputs, original I76 prerequisite records,
1 ms substeps, full saved native preview data, analysis and independent proofs.
It is not a new controller or full 133-second route acceptance. No new physics
or video is generated by packaging this archive.

`project/` holds original repository-relative paths. `_archive/` holds the
pre-archive registration, external prerequisite inventory, explicit omissions,
this guide and the member inventory. Absolute historical provenance paths are
preserved; locate their captured bytes under project/<repository-relative path>.
Do not rewrite historical records to make a different host look original.

All exact sources and inputs from the frozen I81 protocol are included, including
the original I76 eight -1 N precision recording and native plugin/gain/terrain
inputs. The original I76 whole-campaign seal may also mention other episodes;
those unrelated episodes are not claimed to be copied. No virtual environment,
credential helper, unrelated firmware or unrelated user file is included.

Repeated generated workspace meshes/terrain/render caches and interpreter caches
are omitted with path/size/hash/reason inventory. Direct model XML/SDF/config
files remain included. Rebuild generated assets from captured original terrain
and source; new physics on another environment is not promised bit-identical.

Extract into a fresh directory. Start with:

- project/results/slope_response_trace_20261128/protocol/frozen_stage01/protocol.json
- .../campaign/native102_stage01/parity_receipt.json
- .../campaign/native102_stage01/substeps.npz and preview_rows.json
- .../campaign/native102_stage01/child/campaign/runs/eight_m100_N/precision.npz
- .../analysis/ and .../verification/ completed reports and source/input bindings

The output metadata distinguishes inherited I76 job/horizon labels from actual
I81 prefix/capture settings. Each command acts after the row's state; completed
1 ms force samples are not exact solver-impulse measurements. Geometric preview
exposure is not automatically force-onset prediction. Review numerical evidence
and qualifications before making causal claims.

Verify archive members against _archive/archive_inventory.json. The inventory
does not contain its own digest; its hash is separately recorded in the adjacent
archive_verification.json. That receipt records a complete streaming member-hash
check and a final unchanged-source/input/seal/selection check.

Read-only re-analysis uses captured scripts at their original relative paths and
new empty output stages. Consult each registered_before_*.json for exact flags.
The runner requires explicit --run for new physics; no new physics is needed to
inspect the saved data. Keep closed outputs immutable.
