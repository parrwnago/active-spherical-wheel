INTERACTION GUIDE

Take the controls.

Each push applies your selected force for the selected pulse duration. Overlapping pushes add in simulation time; the view shows their actual net force and count. Opposite pushes can cancel. Keyboard controls work when you are outside an input field.

W / SForward / back · world +X / −X
A / DLeft / right · world −Y / +Y
SpacePause or resume
RReset the selected scenario
Drag viewUp / down = ±X · right / left = ±Y

Drag length changes the force up to your slider setting. Use Front view to see transverse tilt at the pole. A trajectory holds its final position when complete. Reset clears the path and starts a new run.

Transition aware feedback follows the current support geometry. Prepare incoming drives matches belt or shell surface speed ahead of a contact change. To compare with Original local LQR, reset with the same tilt and repeat the same pushes. Incoming preparation is inactive in that baseline mode.

The platform is free to turn on its single wheel. Drive reaction torques and ground forces can change its heading; the controller balances without holding a heading angle. Platform spin shows rotation about the world vertical axis.

With belts or the controller off, belt speed is held at zero; frictional contact remains. This ideal speed servo does not model an unpowered motor.
